About the data

__COUNT__ caves from Wikipedia's deepest-caves list, retrieved __DATE__. A selected source snapshot, not a complete world inventory or independently verified ranking.

Reading the comparison

Depth is the vertical span; length is the surveyed passage distance. Green bars show depth and orange bars show length. Each measure uses its own scale, shared across selected caves. Labels remain readable even when a bar is short. Filters affect the list and map; selected caves remain in the comparison.

Locations and access

Map positions are rounded to 0.1° for regional exploration. They are not entrance directions or evidence of permission to visit. Visitor access is unknown. Source region labels are retained, with spacing normalized.
