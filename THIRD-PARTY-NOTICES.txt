Wikipedia-derived catalogue
Data adapted from Wikipedia contributors, List of deepest caves,
revision 1372337231, retrieved 2026-09-09:
https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_deepest_caves&oldid=1372337231
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

whitespace normalized, and published coordinates rounded to 0.1 degrees.
